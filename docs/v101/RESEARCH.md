## Execution/cost assumptions, checked 2026-11-03

Official [TWSE trading mechanism](https://www.twse.com.tw/en/products/system/trading.html) defines a regular-stock trading unit of 1,000 shares; odd-lot sessions differ from the regular opening auction. Challengers use regular 1,000-share lots, not fractional/odd-lot purchases priced at the regular open. Cash not investable in whole lots stays cash. This matters with NT$1m accounts and high-priced Taiwan stocks.

The [TWSE investment guide](https://www.twse.com.tw/zh/about/company/guide.html) documents ordinary stock sell tax of 0.3%; day-trading relief is irrelevant to these multi-day holdings. Commission 0.1425% per side and NT$20 minimum are explicit conservative research assumptions, not a claim of a universal brokerage tariff. Slippage is a separately debited 10 bp per side. Stress doubles commission/slippage, keeping statutory tax unchanged. No free financing, leverage, shorting or dividends are invented.

Size is capped at 1% of trailing 20-session median share volume observed by T close. T+1 daily volume is not used to size an opening trade. Fills reference actual T+1 open and provider observation ID; slippage is a cash cost, not a fabricated market quote. Sells precede buys. A no-trade band never blocks a zero-target risk exit. Capacity-limited residuals remain held and marked; they are not silently liquidated.

## Evidence and hard limitations

Daily official full security tables provide contemporaneous candidate membership rather than a final-date survivor intersection. Current missing holdings, stale feeds, duplicate dates/symbols, malformed bars and missing intermediate sessions block a test; delisting proceeds are never guessed. Nevertheless a complete corporate-action/cash-dividend and delisting-settlement source is not available in the sealed artifact or current V10 provider. Unadjusted-price research cannot pass the corporate-action gate; no total-return or genuinely clean out-of-sample claim is made.

Three challengers are reported alongside an equal-weight momentum control using identical lot/cost/volume/timing assumptions. Original 18-account forward audit is shown separately; differences in period, odd-lot execution and costs prevent treating it as a matched backtest control. All results are paper research, never recommendations or guaranteed profitability. Forward qualification requires at least 60 NEW sessions plus existing risk, data, accounting and stability gates; this PR starts no scheduled forward or real trading.
